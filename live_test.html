Live vision frame test

A one-off check of whether TAB's live-vision page can be shown inside another page, and whether your TAB login carries across. Nothing here reads, stores or sends your login. The frame is TAB's own page running in your own browser. Not linked from the dashboard.

Before you start: log in to tab.com.au in this same browser (open it in another tab first and check you are logged in).

Test 1: TAB live vision inside this page

Open it in a new tab instead

What you see tells us the answer:

  • TAB page, logged in, with a race or video: framing works and your login carries. An integrated player is possible.
  • TAB page asking you to log in: the page frames, but the browser blocks the login cookie inside a frame. Not workable on this device.
  • Blank, or a "refused to connect" message: TAB blocks being framed. Not possible.
